Privacy Policy
----------------

**Last updated: 2026-07-16**

### Introduction
This privacy policy explains what information Jot collects and how it is used.

**Jot does not collect any data.** Jot is an offline, private notes app. It has no accounts, no servers, and no cloud. Everything you write stays on your own device.

### Information Collection and Use
Jot does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. There is no sign-up and no account.

### Where Your Notes Are Stored
- All notes you create are stored **only on your own device**.
- Notes are stored **encrypted with AES-256**. The encryption key is derived on the fly from the passphrase you type, using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.
- Your **passphrase is never stored** and never leaves your device.
- Jot has no cloud sync and no backup servers. Your notes are never uploaded anywhere.

### Third Party Services
Jot contains **no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data**. No third party receives any information from this app.

### Network Access
Jot does not require network access and does not initiate any network connections. Your notes are never transmitted.

### Data Recovery
Because the encryption key is derived from your passphrase and is never stored, **if you forget your passphrase, the notes in that space cannot be recovered** — not by you, and not by us. This is a deliberate design trade-off and part of the privacy guarantee.

### Children's Privacy
Jot does not collect data from users of any age, including children under 13.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.

### Contact Us
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at **lkk54545@gmail.com**.

隱私政策（繁體中文）
----------------

**最後更新：2026-07-16**

### 簡介
**Jot 不蒐集任何資料。** Jot 是一個離線的私密記事本，沒有帳號、沒有伺服器、沒有雲端。你寫的所有內容都留在你自己的裝置上。

### 資料蒐集與使用
Jot **不**蒐集、不儲存、不傳輸任何個人識別資料。我們不要求你的姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人、照片或任何個人資料。無需註冊、沒有帳號。

### 你的筆記存在哪裡
- 你建立的所有筆記**只儲存在你自己的裝置上**。
- 筆記以 **AES-256 加密**儲存，加密金鑰由你輸入的通關字串即時衍生（PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256）。
- 你的**通關字串不會被儲存**，也不會離開你的裝置。
- Jot 沒有雲端同步、沒有備份伺服器，你的筆記不會被上傳到任何地方。

### 第三方服務
Jot **不含廣告、不含分析工具、不含任何會蒐集資料的第三方 SDK**。沒有任何第三方會從本 App 取得資訊。

### 網路存取
Jot 不需要網路連線，也不會主動發起任何連線。你的筆記不會被傳送出去。

### 資料無法救回
由於加密金鑰由你的通關字串衍生且從不保存，**若你忘記通關字串，該空間的筆記將無法救回**——你不行，我們也不行。這是設計上的取捨，也是隱私保障的一部分。

### 兒童隱私
Jot 不向任何年齡的使用者蒐集資料，包含 13 歲以下兒童。

本政策可能不時更新，變更會直接發佈於本頁面並更新上方日期。

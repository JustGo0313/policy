Email us

If something went wrong with a purchase, please include: the Apple receipt or order ID from your email, the date you bought it, and your iOS version. Aquabook has no accounts and no servers, so that receipt is the only thing that identifies your purchase.

Purchases

I paid for Pro but it did not unlock

Open Aquabook Pro (the ribbon icon on the tank list) and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also works on a new phone. If it still does not unlock, email us with the receipt and we will sort it out.

What does Pro actually include?

Unlimited tanks. That is the whole difference. The free version covers one tank with no other limits — unlimited readings, livestock, events and reminders, automatic backup and full data export are all free. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and there is nothing to renew.

The purchase screen says Pro is unavailable

That usually means the App Store could not be reached — check your connection and tap Try again. On a brand-new release it can also mean the purchase is still being reviewed by Apple; it will start working without any update from you.

Your data

Is my data backed up?

From version 1.1, Aquabook keeps one backup in your own iCloud and refreshes it in the background — there is no button to press. It goes to your private iCloud container, not to us: Aquabook has no servers and no account, so we never see your records. You can see the status any time under share icon → iCloud backup.

I got a new phone. How do I get my records back?

Sign in to the same Apple Account, install Aquabook and open it. If a backup is found, it offers to restore before you start. If you skipped that prompt, go to share icon → iCloud backup → Restore.

Can I get my data out of the app?

Yes, and it is free. Tap the share icon on the tank list: Full backup (JSON) is everything in one file, and Water readings / Events (CSV) open directly in any spreadsheet. Your measurements are exported exactly as you entered them.

Aquabook says my tank has no target ranges

Target ranges are not filled in for you — the app never guesses what is right for your tank. Open the flask icon → a parameter and set a range (typical ranges are shown there, and you can apply one with a tap). Until you do, Aquabook will not claim your readings are in range, because it has nothing to compare them to.

Why is there no built-in fish or plant database?

Deliberately. Every species list is wrong for someone, and a list that does not include your fish is worse than no list at all. Everything in Aquabook — parameters, livestock, equipment, event types — is whatever you type in.
